How we grow

A good garden starts with you.

From the first sketch to seasonal care, we make every step feel doable for your space and your schedule.

01 / DESIGN

Space planning

Thoughtful layouts for balconies, rooftops, courtyards, and the in-between spaces.

02 / PLANTING

Garden setup

Climate-friendly plants, healthy soil, and everything you need to get growing.

03 / LEARNING

Hands-on workshops

Learn the simple skills that keep your little patch of green thriving all year.

04 / ONGOING CARE

Seasonal check-ins

Get timely help with pruning, soil refreshes, pest questions, and the next round of planting.

05 / EDIBLE GARDENS

Grow-your-own kits

Start with a space-matched set of seeds, containers, and essentials for herbs and fresh produce.

06 / COMMUNITY

Shared garden projects

Bring neighbors together to plan and plant a courtyard, shared bed, or pollinator-friendly corner.

07 / SOIL HEALTH

Soil & compost coaching

Build healthier growing beds with practical advice on soil, composting, and natural nutrients.

08 / PLANT RESCUE

Plant health visits

Get help identifying common plant troubles and a simple recovery plan for the greenery you love.

09 / SMARTER WATERING

Watering setup

Choose easy, water-wise routines and simple irrigation options that fit your containers and schedule.
